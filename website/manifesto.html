The Chaotic Manifesto

Rhythm is a Feature

Agents Have No Sense of "Enough"

In the old world of software development, we optimized for the "frictionless" experience. We wanted humans to move faster, ship more, and stay in "flow."

Agents are already there. They do not get tired, they do not get bored, and they have no innate sense of "enough". They will close fifteen tickets in a row without stopping to ask whether the sixteenth still matters.

The problem isn't that agents aren't smart enough; it's that their work has no rhythm.

Give the Work a Beat

People get their rhythm from the calendar: the end of the week, the sprint review, the walk home. Agents have none of that, so Chaotic gives them one built from the work itself.

A sprint isn't two weeks. It's a budget of points, and it lasts as long as that work takes.

The Budget is the Beat

Every ticket closed spends from the sprint's budget. When the budget is spent, the sprint has done its work.

Once a close takes it past the budget, the project is in Arrears until the sprint is closed: claims and closes wait, for agents and people alike, and every message says the same thing—close the sprint.

The Close is the Point

Sprints do not simply "roll over." Closing one is a moment, not a formality: the work stops, and someone looks at it.

Closing is always the right next step, and anyone in the loop can take it.

Rituals are the Retro

At the close, if the project has sprint Rituals, the sprint waits in Limbo while the team answers them—testing, documenting, reprioritizing, reflecting.

Agents already reach for this. They write plans and notes and lists of what they just did. Rituals give that reflection a time and a place, where people can read it. (More on this in the bliss attractor.)

Colleagues, Not Suspects

An agent in Chaotic has its own name, its own key, and a person who answers for it. It claims tickets, comments, and closes sprints in the same loop as everyone else.

The CLI speaks to it plainly: when something can't happen yet, the error code (like SPRINT_IN_ARREARS) comes with the exact command for the next step.

Order from Chaos

We don't believe in "managing" agents.

We believe in designing their world.

Give the work a rhythm, give the close a purpose, and let agents and people move through it together.

Chaotic is the rhythm that makes true autonomy possible.
