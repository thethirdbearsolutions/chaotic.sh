The Chaotic app didn't open

Signing in to the Chaotic Android app ends here only when Android hasn't confirmed that the app on this phone is the official one, so it did not hand the sign-in to it. Nothing was signed in; you can close this page.

  • Install or update the app from the official GitHub releases (or Obtainium), with the phone online, then sign in again.
  • If the app came from anywhere else, uninstall it: it is not the official build.

Building the app yourself? Approve the link for your build once (Android 12 or newer):
adb shell pm set-app-links-user-selection --user cur --package sh.chaotic.android true chaotic.sh
